### Function validate_config(cfg) ###

What to validate?

defaults:
    snmp_version (MUST BE present, MUST BE string either "v1", "v2c" or "v3")
    timeout_s (MUST BE present, MUST BE float, MUST BE value 2.5)
    retries (MUST BE present, MUST BE int, MUST BE value 1)
    target_budget_s (MUST BE present, MUST BE int, MUST BE value 10)
    oids (MUST BE present, MUST BE list)
        content of OIDS (MUST BE correct OID names)
